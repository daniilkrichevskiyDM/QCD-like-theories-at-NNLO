This file contains the FORM versions of the masses, decay constants and condensates at NLO and NNLO for the QCD-like theories presented in the paper "QCD-like theories at next-to-next-to-leading order with $N_F=2$ non-degenerate fermions" by J.Bijnens and D.Krichevskiy (2606.16781).

*****Notation******

Here we present the translation of paper notation to code names where the correspondence is not direct.

********************
This file *** Paper
********************
mp2 *** M^2 (for the Sp(4) case) or M_1^2 (for the SO(4) case)
muu *** M_{6}^2 
mdd *** M_{8}^2 
RatioR *** R_q
ABar *** \bar{A}
Hbb *** H^F 
H1bb *** H_{1}^F
H21bb *** H_{21}^F
Hdd *** H^{F\prime}
H1dd *** H^{F\prime}_{1}
H21dd *** H^{F\prime}_{21}
RbarP *** (1 + R_q)^(-1)
RbarM *** (-1 + R_q)^(-1)
